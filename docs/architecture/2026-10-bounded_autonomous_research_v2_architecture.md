## Controlling principle and effective date

> **Infrastructure deployment is not candidate-gated; economic activation is
> candidate-gated.**

The V2 model is:

> Karl approves a bounded research envelope -> a VPS-hosted factory operates
> autonomously inside that envelope -> CPW deterministic machinery owns
> scientific truth -> Hermes reasons over valid evidence and selects the next
> legal bounded action -> humans retain promotion, economic, and live authority.

V2-00 changes future architecture; it does not activate autonomy. Per-hypothesis
approval and every current default-off, no-edge-search, advisory-only, and
no-autonomous-scheduler restriction remain operationally effective until all
of the following are true:

1. Stage 5B is implemented;
2. Stage 5B is validated;
3. Stage 5B is explicitly activated; and
4. a valid Karl-approved bounded research envelope exists.

Until then, Hermes research execution, Prefect autonomous scheduling,
edge-search, and research-factory runtime behavior remain off.

## Bounded research envelope

Karl owns approval, revocation, replacement, and material change of a
versioned, immutable, hash-bound envelope. It must define at least:

- allowed instruments, venues, market types, timeframes, strategy families,
  mechanisms, data-source classes, feature families, and Strategy Spec / DSL
  surface;
- allowed parameter/search domains and Search Governor / Optuna methods;
- per-job, per-hypothesis, and campaign proposal/trial/search budgets, plus
  compute, runtime, concurrency, retry, and recovery limits;
- WFO/OOS, purge/embargo, leakage, robustness, multiple-testing, and
  construction/validation/final-holdout protocols;
- final-holdout exposure and consumption policy;
- deterministic stop conditions, evidence/provenance requirements, Research
  Memory indexing/dedup rules, research dispositions, and human escalation
  conditions.

Automation may not edit, enlarge, reinterpret, replace, or self-authorize the
envelope.

## In-envelope autonomous research after Stage 5B activation

Only after the effective-date conditions are satisfied may the factory,
without separate per-hypothesis approval:

- source and preregister in-envelope hypotheses and Strategy Specs;
- retrieve and deduplicate non-authoritative Research Memory material;
- select an allowed primary timeframe with preregistered rationale;
- construct bounded variants, sweeps, and Search Governor / Optuna plans;
- launch approved jobs through the approved orchestrator;
- perform development, WFO/OOS, robustness, Monte Carlo, deterministic metric,
  and scientific-gate evaluation;
- assign non-candidate research dispositions and terminate weak, duplicate,
  invalid, or insufficient work;
- generate bounded follow-ups, retry/recover within approved limits, produce
  reports and alerts, and schedule the next legal bounded research action;
- fail closed on integrity, safety, scientific-history, or authority failure.

This is research autonomy only. It grants no candidate promotion, execution,
risk, paper/live, or capital authority.

## CPW scientific authority and durable lineage

CPW deterministic machinery remains authoritative for preregistration,
scientific trial identity, denominator/trial accounting, complete population
preservation, WFO/OOS and leakage meaning, purge/embargo, robustness,
multiple-testing/DSR, Search Governor legality, Result Population meaning,
scientific gates, durable lineage, and final-holdout state.

The future authoritative cumulative record must be a CPW-owned durable
scientific-lineage/evidence surface. Stage 5B design must bind at least campaign
and ancestry identity, envelope ID/version/hash, scientific trials and
cumulative budgets, amendments/follow-ups, holdout identity and exposure,
whether holdout information escaped isolation, consumed state,
information-contamination ancestry, and relevant content hashes. V2-00 chooses
no schema, path, storage backend, or writer.

Research Memory may mirror, index, retrieve, and expose this history. It is not
authoritative for whether a trial existed, whether cumulative history counts,
whether a holdout was consumed, or which downstream lineage is contaminated.
Deleting or rebuilding Research Memory, renaming a hypothesis, restarting
Prefect, changing an operational run ID, creating a nominally separate lineage,
branching through Hermes, or starting a child search cannot reset CPW history.

## Final holdout and adaptive research

Every campaign binds three evidence classes: construction/development,
validation/WFO-OOS, and an isolated final holdout. The final holdout is
unavailable to adaptive search until the hypothesis/spec/mechanism, primary
timeframe, search domain, tested artifact, and code/data/envelope identities are
frozen and adaptive search, WFO/OOS, and robustness work are complete.

> **Maximum adaptive final-holdout exposure is one per final-holdout identity
> per information-contamination domain unless strict information isolation is
> proven.**

Once final-holdout information reaches Hermes, a human, Research Memory, Search
Governor, or any other research-influencing component, the holdout is consumed
for every downstream lineage in that contamination domain. A new hypothesis,
lineage, branch, Prefect/run ID, or Research Memory record does not restore
freshness. Later adaptation based directly or indirectly on that exposure
requires a fresh final holdout. Absence of an explicit contamination edge is
not proof of isolation.

An exact operational retry avoids another exposure only when no informative
holdout-derived result escaped the isolated evaluator. The factory must never
perform `holdout result -> adapt -> retest same holdout -> repeat`. Cumulative
multiple-testing/DSR uses the full applicable scientific history, not only the
surviving child search.

## Research dispositions are not candidate lifecycle

Stage 5B must define a closed non-candidate namespace equivalent to:

```text
research_completed
research_completed_no_edge
research_failed
research_terminated
research_duplicate
research_insufficient_evidence
research_blocked
```

An automated research disposition is not rejection, promotion, readiness,
candidate lifecycle transition, paper/live action, or economic decision.
`research_completed_no_edge` does not mean candidate `rejected`, and
`research_completed` does not mean `validated_research`. Interesting evidence
may surface a human candidate-review request; it cannot mutate lifecycle state.

## Fail-safe stop and recovery authority

The factory always owns the machine-safe ability to stop further research
effects when legality, bounds, or evidence honesty cannot be proven. It must
fail closed for invalid/revoked/stale envelope identity, out-of-envelope work,
exhausted budgets, holdout-policy conflict, provenance or integrity failure,
missing or contradictory inputs, preregistration mismatch, irreconcilable
history, exhausted retry, idempotency conflict, protected-boundary contact, or
unsafe/invalid work.

Automatic recovery is permitted only when the envelope pre-approves bounded
retryable error classes, exact-input/same-trial retry, deterministic resume,
worker restart, or queue recovery. Recovery may not change science, expand
scope or budget, create another holdout exposure, or alter the envelope.

Human authorization remains required to change or reissue the envelope,
override budgets, expand search scope, change scientific or gate policy, accept
contradictory evidence, resume after an authority/protected-boundary violation,
perform policy-controlled protected-runtime reset/recovery, change risk, or
cause paper/live/exchange/capital effects.

## Permanent human gates

Humans retain authority for:

1. creating, replacing, expanding, revoking, or materially changing the envelope;
2. adding any out-of-envelope instrument, venue, market type, timeframe, data
   or feature family, strategy mechanism, search domain, or compute authority;
3. changing scientific policy, trial/denominator meaning, preregistration,
   WFO/OOS/leakage, holdout, robustness, multiple-testing, Search Governor, or
   gate-threshold semantics;
4. candidate promotion and research-to-shadow transition;
5. shadow-to-paper, paper/live, and other protected execution transitions;
6. risk policy/limits and required protected-runtime kill/reset/recovery;
7. capital allocation/material expansion and live activation.

Ordinary legal in-envelope research creation, execution, scientific failure,
termination, and follow-up are not per-job gates after Stage 5B activation.

## External-system ownership

- **CPW** owns domain/scientific legality, evidence semantics, durable lineage,
  candidate meaning, hard risk, reconciliation safety policy, approvals, and
  canonical writes.
- **Hermes** may reason over valid evidence and select legal bounded research
  actions, but cannot manufacture PASS, reset history, change the envelope,
  promote, change risk, activate trading, or allocate capital.
- **Research Memory** is a non-authoritative index/retrieval and dedup surface.
- **Search Governor** owns deterministic bounded-search legality; **Optuna**
  proposes only within its approved space. Neither owns evidence or promotion.
- **Prefect** may own generic flow/task, scheduling, retry, concurrency, and
  operational state after cutover. It does not own scientific identity,
  holdout state, approval, promotion, risk, or capital.
- **Humans** retain every permanent gate above.

## V2 roadmap and parallel operation

V2-00 is the Stage-5A-equivalent authority lock. There is no second future
architecture-lock stage. After V2-00 merges, implementation remains serialized:

```text
five-timeframe/data-feature foundation
-> Prefect real-consumer orchestration
-> Hermes + Research Memory + Search Governor/Optuna integration
-> product bridge/runner/WFO-OOS/robustness/candidate-review integration
-> Stage 5B envelope enforcement + explicit autonomous-research activation
-> VPS hosting/security/deployment
-> VPS always-on acceptance
=> continuous non-capital research operation
```

VPS deployment does not wait for a genuine candidate. After always-on
acceptance, Stage 8A+ serialized engineering (Nautilus N1-02, N2, N3 and later
protected integration) and Stage 8R continuous research operate in parallel.
Candidate discovery may occur before, during, or after those engineering
packages. A genuine candidate reaching the scientific boundary is preserved
there until downstream human-gated infrastructure is ready; research continues
for stronger, complementary, regime-specific, or replacement candidates.

One genuine candidate satisfying the scientific boundary may enter human-gated
paper/shadow qualification when downstream infrastructure is ready. A perfect
or final strategy is not required. Real-capital activation still requires
scientific evidence, paper/shadow evidence, protected runtime readiness,
risk/reconciliation/safety proof, and explicit Karl approval. Initial capital
is small and staged under separate authority.
