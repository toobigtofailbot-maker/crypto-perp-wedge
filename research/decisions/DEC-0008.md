# DEC-0008: H-008 Baseline — Premium-Confirmed Momentum Continuation

## Summary

H-008 tested whether adding 1h premium index confirmation to price momentum
selectivity would salvage the 1h intraday layer after H-005's NO-GO verdict.
The baseline result is **NO-GO**: negative Sharpe, catastrophic drawdown, and
no signal edge even before fees.

## Hypothesis

**H-008: Premium-Confirmed Momentum Continuation**

- Primary timeframe: 1h
- Entry: `price_momentum[t] > 0` AND `premium_z[t] > 1.0` → LONG;
  `price_momentum[t] < 0` AND `premium_z[t] < -1.0` → SHORT
- Exit long: `premium_z[t] <= 0` OR `exit_momentum[t] < 0`; reverse if opposite entry met
- Exit short: `premium_z[t] >= 0` OR `exit_momentum[t] > 0`; reverse if opposite entry met
- Parameters: `momentum_lookback=24`, `premium_lookback=168`, `premium_z_threshold=1.0`, `exit_lookback=6`

H-008 was **worse** than H-005 on every metric except the mechanical cost drag gate (Gate 5 passes when gross PnL is negative because the ratio is defined as cost/gross, which flips sign). The signal had no raw edge at all (negative gross PnL) compared to H-005's modestly positive gross PnL before costs.

### Why H-008 Failed

1. **Trade count explosion:** The `exit_lookback=6` exit condition triggered frequent exits
   followed by rapid re-entries, producing 5.6× more trades than H-005. The premium z-score
   filter (30.4% permissive) did not control this churn.

2. **No underlying edge:** Gross PnL was -$91,802 (before fees). The signal produced more
   losing trades than winning ones (hit rate 37.4%, profit factor 0.71). Premium confirmation
   added a second requirement for entry but did not improve the underlying directional accuracy
   of simple momentum.

3. **Drawdown catastrophic:** Max DD of 3928.85% reflects negative final equity (-$392,137
   from $10,000 initial). The account was destroyed by a combination of bad signals and fees.

4. **Parameter instability:** The sensitivity analysis shows that changing exit_lookback from
   6 to 4 flips Sharpe from -0.15 to +0.45 — a sign reversal from a 25% parameter shift.
   This indicates the baseline parameters happen to be near a knife edge rather than a stable
   configuration.

5. **Premium z-score is a noisy filter:** At 30.4% permissivity (similar to the proportion
   of bars where the filter is active), the threshold of 1.0 standard deviations is too
   loose to provide meaningful selectivity over simple momentum.

## Decision

**Archive as NO-GO.** H-008 hypothesis is CONCLUDED.

The premium confirmation layer did not salvage 1h momentum. The independent derivatives
stream (premium index) adds another entry requirement but does not identify entry points
with higher directional accuracy than the price-only H-005 baseline.

## Rationale

- Gross PnL is negative, meaning no amount of cost reduction would produce a profitable strategy.
- The exit momentum condition creates high churn that overwhelms any benefit from the premium filter.
- Sensitivity analysis shows the parameters are near a sign-reversal boundary — the baseline
  result is not stable even within ±25% parameter shifts.
- The failure mode is qualitatively different from H-005 (which had positive gross PnL but was
  destroyed by fees on 934 trades): H-008 has no edge whatsoever.

## Comparison to H-005 Failure Mode

H-005 failed primarily due to cost drag (445% of gross PnL) overwhelming a modestly positive
gross signal. H-008 fails for a more fundamental reason: negative gross PnL. The premium
confirmation filter did not help; if anything, the complex entry/exit logic increased churn.
