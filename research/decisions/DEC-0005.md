## Key Observations

- The baseline produced a positive full-period Sharpe (0.3751), but it remains well below the 1.0 promotion threshold.
- Max drawdown reached 212.0803%, breaching both promotion and kill thresholds.
- Cost drag was 445.5331% of gross PnL, showing the 1h structure-confirmed MACD baseline is overwhelmed by turnover and modeled costs.
- Both long and short legs were negative on net PnL, with the short leg carrying most of the loss.

## Portfolio Context Note

This result is evidence about one 1h intraday momentum candidate, not about the entire intraday research direction. The 1h timeframe has one failed baseline cycle, so it should not enter Cold Storage under D2-017 yet. However, the observed cost drag and drawdown argue for caution before expanding to faster 15m momentum variants.

## Decision

**NO-GO**

## Rationale

H-005 fails the required promotion gates and triggers pre-registered kill criteria through max drawdown above 35%, cost drag above 50% of gross PnL, and both long and short legs being unprofitable. The structure-confirmed hourly MACD baseline does not justify shadow promotion or additional tuning under the same hypothesis.

## Next-Step Recommendation

Archive H-005 as a baseline NO-GO. Future 1h momentum work should require a materially different rationale for turnover control, regime selection, or exit design before another hypothesis is registered.

## 15m Implication

H-005 does not support immediate 15m momentum expansion. The faster 15m layer remains data-ready, but this result suggests the next 15m package should be cautious about cost drag, trade frequency, and signal fragility rather than registering a mechanically faster MACD-style candidate.
