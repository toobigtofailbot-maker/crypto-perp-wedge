## Owner target

> **Extreme Hermes is the intended non-capital research-factory intelligence,
> research director, and continuous-improvement brain.**

Extreme Hermes is intended to be persistent and always on inside an externally
isolated research environment. It is not merely a chatbot or one-shot request
generator. Its eventual research role includes persistent sessions and
context, web and browser research, sandboxed terminal/Python/file use, repo and
Git inspection, explicitly exposed GitHub capabilities, reusable research
skills, specialist delegation, parallel critical review, scheduled and
wake-driven supervision, Research Memory, CPW research/evidence interfaces,
Prefect workflows, observability, data- and strategy-health monitoring,
research diagnosis, alerts, briefs, reviews, and selection of the next legal
in-envelope research action.

The current 01A one-shot, Project-Evidence-only, 14-tool deployment is a proven
safety/control baseline. It is not Hermes's permanent ceiling.

## Broad capability sandbox; narrow authority gateway

> **Hermes can control the laboratory. It cannot control the vault.**

Hermes may eventually have broad capability freedom and may mutate substantial
research-sandbox state where a later package explicitly enables it. Its
authority remains narrow across scientific, protected-runtime, and economic
boundaries. Tool allowlists, prompts, and SOUL instructions are useful
defense-in-depth; they are not the primary security boundary and do not replace
CPW-owned authority checks or external isolation.

## Target operating loop

The intended post-Stage-5B loop is:

```text
Karl approves a broad immutable research envelope
-> Hermes continuously discovers research opportunities
-> Hermes uses specialists, Research Memory, external research and repo evidence
-> CPW checks envelope legality, scientific policy and durable lineage
-> Search Governor constrains legal search and scientific identity/budgets
-> Optuna proposes legal points where applicable
-> Prefect executes operational research workflows
-> CPW produces/evaluates deterministic scientific evidence
-> Hermes interprets and diagnoses the evidence
-> Hermes selects/proposes the next legal in-envelope research action
-> loop continues automatically
```

Only after Stage 5B is implemented, validated, explicitly activated, and a
valid Karl-approved immutable envelope exists may ordinary legal in-envelope
hypotheses, parameter searches, diagnosis, follow-up, and non-candidate
research dispositions proceed without separate Karl approval per job.
Out-of-envelope work remains blocked and human-gated.

## Project freedom levels

### F0 — bounded proof baseline

The current 01A-style posture is manual/one-shot with an exact restricted MCP
surface and without broad browser, web, terminal, file, code, memory, or
delegation capability or autonomous successor research. Its purpose is
safety/control proof, regression control, canary fallback, and fail-safe
fallback. It is not the permanent target.

### F1 — broad isolated research sandbox

Hermes gains web/browser research, terminal/Python, sandbox files and scratch,
repo inspection, memory/session continuity, and reusable skills inside an
isolated environment. It still has no autonomous scientific execution
authority.

### F2 — multi-agent scientist

Hermes gains specialist and critical-review agents, parallel research roles,
data/statistics/robustness/software specialists, and shared sandbox research
coordination. It still cannot bypass CPW or Search Governor authority.

### F3 — continuous lab supervisor

Hermes gains persistent and wake-driven supervision, schedules, data-health,
strategy-health and lab-bottleneck monitoring, alerts, daily/weekly reporting,
and external research scouting. This level does not authorize autonomous
scientific experiments outside Stage 5B.

### F4 — autonomous scientific operator

After the autonomy kernel exists, Hermes may operate through envelope-derived
policy, durable lineage, Search Governor/Optuna, and Prefect to launch legal
in-envelope experiments, inspect results, and perform bounded follow-up. It has
no candidate-promotion or economic authority.

### F5 — Extreme Hermes autonomous factory

After full research-loop integration and Stage 5B activation, Hermes may
continuously `discover -> test -> evaluate -> diagnose -> choose next legal
action -> repeat`, including WFO/OOS, robustness, Monte Carlo, deterministic
evidence generation/evaluation, Research Memory indexing/context, legal
follow-up, and persistent multi-agent supervision. This is the primary Extreme
Hermes target for the non-capital research factory.

### F6 — autonomous lab engineer

In a later separately authorized package, Hermes may edit non-protected
research tooling in disposable branches/worktrees, run tests, improve research
skills/workflows, analyze diffs, and prepare PRs. Human review and merge remain
mandatory. Protected paths remain outside Hermes write authority.

### F7 — paper/live observer-advisor

Later, Hermes may receive broad read-only and diagnostic visibility into mature
paper/live operations for incident analysis, strategy-health and
execution-quality advice, and human-review proposals. It receives no direct
live or economic authority.

## Proof-only restrictions prospectively superseded

The following 01A-era restrictions are proof-only and must not be interpreted
as permanent architecture:

- one-shot invocation as the only Hermes mode;
- an exact 14-tool ceiling as a permanent limit;
- Project-Evidence-only interaction as the permanent tool architecture;
- browser, web, and search disabled forever;
- terminal, Python, code execution, and file editing disabled forever;
- persistent memory and session search disabled forever;
- skills and self-improvement disabled forever;
- delegation and specialist agents disabled forever;
- cron, scheduled, and wake-driven work disabled forever;
- autonomous legal successor research disabled forever.

These capabilities remain operationally disabled until later packages prove
and authorize them. Prospective supersession does not enable them.

## Permanent restrictions

Unless a later explicit owner decision changes them, Hermes must never
unilaterally:

- manufacture or rewrite scientific truth or convert FAIL into PASS;
- erase prior trials or cumulative search history;
- reset scientific lineage by rename, restart, new session, or new agent;
- treat consumed or contaminated holdout information as fresh;
- bypass Search Governor legality or budgets;
- manufacture scientific-trial meaning or alter locked scientific/gate policy;
- promote a strategy into candidate, shadow, paper, or live state;
- alter protected hard-risk policy;
- override OMS, reconciliation, safe-mode, or kill-switch authority;
- bypass protected recovery/reset policy;
- hold unrestricted live-exchange or capital-bearing credentials;
- authorize live trading or allocate real capital.

CPW deterministic machinery and human gates remain authoritative.

## External isolation and security architecture

Extreme Hermes must be secured primarily outside the model/runtime
configuration. The target requires:

- a dedicated research container, VM, or VPS boundary;
- filesystem separation and explicit protected repo-path separation;
- no protected-path writable mount by default;
- a sandbox-only writable research workspace;
- disposable worktrees for later code-improvement mode;
- credential isolation, with no unrestricted live-exchange or capital-bearing
  credentials;
- controlled network/egress policy and explicit provider/secrets handling;
- controlled CPW authority APIs/gateways;
- bounded compute, concurrency, and runtime quotas;
- observability and audit trails;
- kill/disable capability independent of Hermes; and
- fail-closed behavior whenever authority cannot be established.

Before `EXTREME-HERMES-01` enables broad tools, an independent security review
must challenge the sandbox/container/VM boundary, mounts, protected-path
separation, network/egress controls, credential model, tool-permission
enforcement, delegation/subagent containment, worktree/code-write containment,
and upstream Hermes security/version regressions.

## Research-code freedom

At F6 / `EXTREME-HERMES-03`, Hermes may write non-protected research code,
improve lab utilities and skills, run tests, inspect diffs, and prepare PRs only
inside disposable branches/worktrees or equivalent isolated code workspaces.
It may not self-merge, write protected execution/risk/OMS/reconciliation paths,
bypass protected-path policy, deploy live changes, or authorize capital.

## Version-upgrade policy

This architecture locks a selection policy, not a replacement version. The
accepted proof deployment remains pinned to `v2026.8.13 / v0.20.1`. The
package's authorization snapshot identified `v2026.9.14 / v0.21.3` as the
latest GitHub release and `v2026.9.21 / v0.21.4` as a later tag. An
execution-time official GitHub API check on 2026-10-06 instead reported
`v2026.9.24 / v0.21.5` as the latest release. This drift is evidence that no
future pin belongs in this architecture lock.

Before broad capability enablement, `EXTREME-HERMES-01` must independently
refresh official release/tag truth; select and pin an exact reviewed stable
tag, version, image, and digest; review changes from the proof pin; canary the
upgrade in isolation; validate session/state, browser/web,
terminal/Python/files, memory/skills/delegation/cron, MCP/tool boundaries,
credentials, egress, provider/runtime stability, and rollback; and run a
capability/security regression. A tag's existence alone is insufficient.

as Hermes's permanent operating model. It must not default to one-tool-at-a-time
permission packages unless a concrete security or compatibility dependency
requires that split. The project should follow the shortest safe path to broad
research capability while preserving narrow scientific, protected-runtime,
and economic authority.

research, Stage 5B, code-writing role, protected runtime, paper/live access,
