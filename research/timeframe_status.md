## Cold Storage Rules (D2-017)

A timeframe enters cold storage if after two hypothesis cycles no candidate
survives the D2-006 promotion gates. Cold-storage timeframes:

* Remain fetchable
* Are not routinely tested
* Require a new hypothesis with explicit economic rationale to revisit
* Trials on revisited timeframes still count toward cumulative trial count for DSR
