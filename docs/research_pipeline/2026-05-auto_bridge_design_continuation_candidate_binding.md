## F. Candidate-Binding Posture

The AUTO bridge architecture design remains candidate-agnostic for this design
tranche.

Candidate-agnostic means the bridge design describes
source/provenance/transformation/canonical-boundary requirements without
selecting or binding to any candidate, cohort, A2C path, Path B path, or future
evidence track.

Candidate-agnostic design preserves portability across possible future cohorts
or evidence tracks.

Candidate-agnostic design avoids coupling the bridge to A2C-001, which remains
stopped/deferred.

Candidate-agnostic design avoids premature H-011 first-time inclusion, H-012+
registration, cohort modification, Path B authorization, or performance-based
selection.

## G. What Candidate-Agnostic Does Not Mean

Candidate-agnostic design does not authorize a bridge implementation.

It does not authorize candidate registration.

It does not authorize future candidate selection.

It does not authorize generic evidence acceptance.

It does not authorize cross-candidate or cross-segment inference.

It does not authorize candidate-level, selected-candidate, or cohort-level
approval.

It does not decide which canonical artifact family the bridge targets.

It does not decide whether existing AUTO outputs may ever be considered.

It does not decide schema/validator/query-layer authority.

It does not decide implementation-tranche scope.
