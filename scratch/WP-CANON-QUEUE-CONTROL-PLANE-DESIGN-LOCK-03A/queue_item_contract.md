# Queue-item contract

Normative schema: `research/schemas/canon_queue_item.schema.json`. Status is permanently `admitted_non_dispatched`; target is `offline_research`; priority is bounded; content has no command, runner, scheduler, provider, paper/live, or promotion surface. ID, target, Gate-Q approval, hashes, denominator, dataset, budget, lineage, and immutable posture must agree exactly.
