5. Locked decisions

7. Active workstream and current status
8. Open questions
9. Important reasoning to preserve
10. Relevant files / artifacts / branches / reports
11. Validation status
12. Narrowest recommended next step
13. Paste-ready bootstrap prompt for the new chat
14. Handoff confidence and risk

For each important item, label it as one of:
- confirmed
- inferred
- open
- superseded
- needs validation

Optimize for continuation quality, not brevity.
