## Context

H-007 tested the pre-registered Funding + Momentum Composite baseline on BTCUSDT USDT-M perpetual 4h bars. The baseline used the registered 6-bar rolling funding mean, 0.0001 funding threshold, 12-bar close-to-close momentum, and zero momentum threshold against the full catalogued 4h OHLCV and 8h funding histories.

No separate IS/OOS split convention exists in repo truth. Per the current runbook baseline pattern, this baseline records the full-period Sharpe in both the in-sample and OOS trial-log fields; later walk-forward packages should replace this with a formal split/fold plan.

Funding was aligned to the 4h OHLCV bar-close grid by forward-fill for signal construction. Before calling `run_backtest()`, the notebook reduces the funding DataFrame to events that can occur while the generated signal is in position. This preserves funding cashflows while avoiding irrelevant funding-event scans in the current backtester implementation.

## Key Observations

- The composite produced 240 trades, with 1151 short-signal bars and 119 long-signal bars. Short exposure dominated the baseline.
- Full-period Sharpe was negative and below the promotion threshold, so the funding-plus-momentum confirmation did not repair the H-001 funding failure mode.
- Funding cashflows were favorable in aggregate, but modeled fees and slippage overwhelmed the small gross PnL base.
- Long trades were positive on net, while the short leg was materially negative; the overall strategy was not balanced enough to justify tuning.

## Portfolio Context Note

This result is evidence about one funding-composite 4h candidate, not a rejection of all derivative-market data streams. It does, however, add another failed 4h cycle after H-001 and H-002, so the 4h timeframe needs stronger evidence before further routine testing.

## Decision

**NO-GO**

## Rationale

H-007 fails the required promotion gates and triggers pre-registered kill criteria through OOS Sharpe below 0.3, max drawdown above 35%, cost drag above 50% of gross PnL, and failure to materially improve on the H-001 funding-directional failure mode. The baseline does not justify shadow promotion or additional tuning under the same hypothesis.

## Implications for H-008 / H-009 / Future Work

H-008 and H-009 remain in scope for WP-4b2 because they test different 1h and 30m families. Future funding-family work should require a materially different risk-control or selectivity mechanism rather than another direct funding-directional composite.
