## Context

H-006 tested the pre-registered 30-Minute Bollinger Band Squeeze Breakout baseline on BTCUSDT USDT-M perpetual 30m bars. The baseline used the registered 20-period Bollinger Bands, 20th percentile squeeze threshold, ATR(14) stop framing, 1.5 ATR stop distance, 2R target distance, and 20-bar time stop against the full catalogued 30m OHLCV history.

No separate IS/OOS split convention exists in repo truth. Per the current runbook baseline pattern, this baseline records the full-period Sharpe in both the in-sample and OOS trial-log fields; later walk-forward packages should replace this with a formal split/fold plan.

Full funding history was loaded from the catalogued funding parquet. Before calling `run_backtest()`, the notebook reduces that DataFrame to funding events that can occur while the generated signal is in position. This preserves funding cashflows while avoiding irrelevant funding-event scans in the current intraday backtester implementation.

## Key Observations

- The baseline produced negative full-period Sharpe (-0.3512), failing the primary promotion gate and triggering the OOS Sharpe kill criterion.
- Max drawdown reached 612.0835%, breaching both promotion and kill thresholds.
- Both long and short legs were negative on net PnL, indicating that the squeeze breakout framing did not produce durable follow-through.
- Gate 5 passes mechanically under the current evaluator because gross PnL is negative, making cost-drag fraction negative. This pass should not be interpreted as healthy cost behavior.

## Portfolio Context Note

This result is evidence about one 30m intraday volatility-breakout candidate, not about the entire intraday research direction. The 30m timeframe has one failed baseline cycle, so it should not enter Cold Storage under D2-017 yet. However, the result warns that shorter intraday breakouts are highly exposed to noise, drawdown, and modeled cost burden.

## Decision

**NO-GO**

## Rationale

H-006 fails the required promotion gates and triggers pre-registered kill criteria through OOS Sharpe below 0.3 and max drawdown above 35%. The 30m squeeze breakout baseline does not justify shadow promotion or additional tuning under the same hypothesis.

## Next-Step Recommendation

Archive H-006 as a baseline NO-GO. Future 30m breakout work should require a materially different rationale for selectivity, risk containment, or regime filtering before another hypothesis is registered.

## 15m Implication

H-006 argues against immediate 15m breakout expansion. The faster 15m layer remains data-ready, but this result supports a cautious next package focused on stronger economic rationale and cost/noise controls before testing shorter-horizon variants.
