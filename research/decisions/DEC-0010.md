## Context

H-010 tested the pre-registered Basis / Premium Mean Reversion baseline on BTCUSDT USDT-M perpetual 4h bars. The baseline computed mark-index basis, normalized it with a 90-bar z-score, required premium-index directional consistency for entries, exited near neutral, and enforced an 18-bar max holding period.

No separate IS/OOS split convention exists in repo truth. Per the current runbook baseline pattern, this baseline records the full-period Sharpe in both the in-sample and OOS trial-log fields; later walk-forward packages should replace this with a formal split/fold plan.

4h OHLCV was used as the executable trading price stream. Mark price, index price, and premium index klines were aligned to the 4h OHLCV bar-close grid for signal construction. The current irreparable-gap manifest has no 4h mark, index, or premium entries, and no H-010 signal bars were skipped by D2-003A.

## Key Observations

- Basis z-score exceeded +2.0 on 549 bars and fell below -2.0 on 367 bars; the premium consistency check left 359 short-entry condition bars and 304 long-entry condition bars.
- The baseline produced positive full-period PnL and Sharpe, but Sharpe remained below the 1.0 promotion threshold.
- Max drawdown reached 84.9723%, which is far above both the D2-006 gate and the pre-registered 35% kill framing.
- Cost drag reached 75.8594% of gross PnL, so the structural-dislocation signal was not strong enough to absorb modeled execution friction.
- The +25% basis-z threshold variant flipped Sharpe negative, making the baseline too parameter-sensitive for Gate 4.

## Portfolio Context Note

H-010 is the strongest of the two WP-4b1 4h cross-stream baselines, but it still fails promotion. Positive PnL is not sufficient because the drawdown, cost drag, and sensitivity profile are not suitable for shadow promotion.

## Decision

**NO-GO**

## Rationale

H-010 fails the required promotion gates and triggers pre-registered kill criteria through max drawdown above 35%, cost drag above 50% of gross PnL, and evidence that basis-threshold behavior is fragile. The baseline does not justify shadow promotion or additional tuning under the same hypothesis.

## Implications for Future Basis-Family Work

Future basis or premium mean-reversion hypotheses should focus on materially different risk containment, lower-cost execution assumptions only if justified by repo truth, or a stricter regime filter. The current result does not support another near-variant of the same 4h basis-z entry rule.
