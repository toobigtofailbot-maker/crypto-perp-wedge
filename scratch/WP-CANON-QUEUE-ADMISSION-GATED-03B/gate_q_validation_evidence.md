Each record locates its stored item and authoritative Gate-Q file, which are fed through the same complete validator; the regenerated queue/Gate-Q/core/Spec/VM/Sweep/timestamp/no-authority evidence must exactly match the audit line. Missing or changed dataset, Gate-C, approval, draft, review, or eligibility evidence also fails through that validator.
