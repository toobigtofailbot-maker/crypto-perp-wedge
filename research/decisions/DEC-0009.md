# DEC-0009: H-009 Baseline — Multi-Timeframe Trend Alignment

## Summary

H-009 tested whether requiring both 1h and 4h trend alignment before entering
on a 30m breakout trigger would provide sufficient filtering to overcome the
cost-drag failure mode observed in H-006. The baseline result is **NO-GO**:
negative Sharpe, 925% drawdown, and insufficient filter selectivity (78% of
bars permitted entries) that failed to control turnover.

## Hypothesis

**H-009: Multi-Timeframe Trend Alignment**

- Primary timeframe: 30m
- Confirmation timeframes: 1h, 4h
- Entry: `close_30m > trigger_high_30m` AND `trend_1h == +1` AND `trend_4h == +1` → LONG;
  `close_30m < trigger_low_30m` AND `trend_1h == -1` AND `trend_4h == -1` → SHORT
- Exit: 1h or 4h trend no longer agrees, opposite 30m trigger fires,
  ATR stop (`atr_multiplier × ATR(14)`), or time stop (`time_stop_bars` bars)
- Parameters: `trigger_lookback_30m=20`, `trend_lookback_1h=48`, `trend_lookback_4h=30`,
  `atr_multiplier=2.0`, `time_stop_bars=24`
- Causal alignment: 1h and 4h trend states forward-filled to 30m index via union+ffill
  (no look-ahead)

## Observations

### Comparison to H-006 (30m Baseline)

| Metric | H-006 (30m BB squeeze) | H-009 (multi-TF) |
|--------|------------------------|------------------|
| Sharpe | -0.3512 | -0.1638 |
| Max Drawdown | 612.08% | 925.16% |
| Total Trades | 926 | **2,148 (2.3×)** |

| Gross PnL | -$27,929 | **+$15,592** |
| Cost Drag | -194.29% (mech.) | 864.96% |

H-009 improved Sharpe marginally relative to H-006 and produced positive gross PnL
(unlike H-006's negative gross), but:
- Trade count doubled (2148 vs 926), making cost drag worse
- Max drawdown increased (925% vs 612%)
- The multi-TF filter at 78% permissivity was nearly as permissive as having no filter

### Why H-009 Failed

1. **Insufficient filter selectivity:** With 78% of bars permitting entry (both 1h and 4h
   trends agree), the multi-TF filter provides almost no reduction in signal frequency
   compared to a single-timeframe approach. The trend alignment was too often concurrent
   for this to act as a meaningful gate.

2. **Cost drag remains dominant:** Even though gross PnL was positive (+$15,592), fees
   ($86,620) and slippage ($43,310) produced total costs of $129,930 — 8.65× gross PnL.
   At 2148 trades, the per-trade cost burden is structurally too high for 30m timeframes.

3. **Extreme parameter instability:** Most ±25% parameter shifts flip the Sharpe sign from
   negative to positive. This is not a stable failure — the signal concept has some
   directional content, but the specific default parameters happen to be at a local minimum.
   The hypothesis as specified cannot be promoted; sensitivity analysis does not support
   any consistent edge.

4. **Time and ATR stops did not help:** The ATR and time stops (atr_multiplier=2.0,
   time_stop_bars=24) were designed to limit drawdowns, but the 78% filter permissivity
   means too many trades are entered for the stops to provide systemic protection.

### Multi-TF Alignment Impact

The multi-TF alignment filter (requiring 1h and 4h agreement) did reduce trade count
compared to a pure 30m breakout (2,148 vs H-006's 926 with 30m BB squeeze is somewhat
misleading since H-009 uses a different signal structure). However, 78% permissivity
confirms the filter is too weak — the 1h and 4h trends at 48-bar and 30-bar MAs are
almost always aligned, making the filter nearly redundant.

The sensitivity result where trend_lookback_1h=36 bars (shorter MA) produces Sharpe=+0.87
suggests that shorter trend lookbacks — which disagree more often with 4h — could improve
selectivity. But this would require a different hypothesis specification.

## Decision

**Archive as NO-GO.** H-009 hypothesis is CONCLUDED.

The multi-timeframe alignment filter at the specified parameters did not provide sufficient
selectivity to overcome the 30m cost drag failure mode from H-006.

## Rationale

- Cost drag at 864.96% of gross PnL is 6 gates above the 50% threshold.
- Filter permissivity of 78% is too high to provide meaningful noise reduction.
- Gross PnL was positive (+$15,592), confirming some raw directional content, but the
  strategy would need cost drag reduced by ~17× to pass Gate 5 at current trade counts.
- Sensitivity analysis shows extreme instability — the baseline parameters are a local
  minimum, not a stable negative result. The hypothesis in its specified form cannot be
  promoted regardless of tuning at ±25% shifts.

## Comparison to H-006 Failure Mode

H-006 failed with negative gross PnL and extreme drawdown (612%). H-009 improved on both
(positive gross PnL, higher but different drawdown pattern) but failed because the multi-TF
filter was too permissive to reduce trade frequency. Both 30m candidates ultimately fail
on cost drag and drawdown, confirming that 30m intraday strategies face structural
cost obstacles at current Binance fee/slippage assumptions.

## Implications for 15m Expansion

H-009 does not support 15m expansion. If two 30m hypothesis cycles (H-006, H-009) both
produce NO-GO verdicts, expanding to 15m — a faster timeframe with even higher cost drag
exposure — would face worse structural headwinds. Current intraday evidence argues against
15m expansion until either: (a) a cost reduction mechanism is identified, or (b) a 15m
hypothesis with fundamentally lower turnover requirements is designed.
