# H-FUNDING-RATE-POSITIONING-FADE-01

Extreme positive funding implies long-side crowding and a contrarian short entry. Extreme negative funding implies short-side crowding and a contrarian long entry. The frozen family is 3 rolling lookbacks × 2 symmetric tail pairs × 2 exit modes × 3 maximum holds = 36 rows. The test uses price P&L with existing costs; funding payments remain off. No post-result variant or threshold was added.
